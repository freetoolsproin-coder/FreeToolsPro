Great AI output usually starts with a **great prompt**—not a longer model. The best prompts share structure: role, goal, context, constraints, and format.

## Prompt patterns that travel well

### 1. Role + task
“You are a patient tutor. Explain [topic] to a first-year student in 5 short paragraphs with one analogy.”

### 2. Context sandwich
Paste the source material, then: “Using only the text above, summarize… Do not invent facts.”

### 3. Constraints
“Max 120 words. No buzzwords. End with 3 bullet action items.”

### 4. Few-shot examples
Show 1–2 sample inputs/outputs, then ask for the same style on a new case.

### 5. Critique loop
“List weaknesses in this draft, then rewrite fixing those points.”

## Starter prompts worth saving

- **Email:** “Rewrite this email to be polite, shorter, and clear about the ask: …”
- **Code:** “Explain this function line by line, then suggest one safer edge-case check: …”
- **Study:** “Quiz me with 8 questions on [topic]; wait for my answers before revealing solutions.”
- **Resume:** “Turn these bullets into achievement statements with metrics where plausible: …”
