AI can be a tutor, not a ghostwriter. The difference is whether **you** still do the thinking your grade is meant to measure.

## High-value student uses

- Explain a concept three ways until it clicks
- Generate practice quizzes (answers hidden until you attempt)
- Outline essays from your notes—not from thin air
- Summarize *your* lecture notes for revision
- Check grammar after you drafted the ideas

## Risky / often disallowed uses

- Submitting AI text as your own where policy forbids it
- Feeding paywalled exam PDFs into tools that store chats
- Invented citations that look real

Always follow your school’s AI policy.

## Study prompt sketch

“Act as a tutor. Ask me one question at a time about [topic]. After each answer, correct me briefly and ask the next question. After 8 questions, summarize what I still miss.”

Improve that prompt with the [AI Prompt Optimizer](/social-media-tools/ai-prompt-optimizer).

## Writing workflow

1. Brain dump your thesis and evidence.
2. Use the [AI Essay Writer](/social-media-tools/ai-essay-writer) only as a structure aid if your course allows.
3. Rewrite in your voice; add citations you actually opened.
4. Optionally scan for generic AI patterns with the [AI Content Detector](/trending-tools/ai-content-detector)—detectors are imperfect; your integrity is not.

## Mindset

If AI removes struggle that builds skill, you borrowed the grade. If AI removes busywork so you practice harder problems, you invested well.
