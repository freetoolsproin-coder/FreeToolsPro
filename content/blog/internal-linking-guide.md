Internal links are how you declare **which pages matter** and how topics relate. They help users move, help crawlers discover URLs, and distribute relevance across your site.

## Principles that scale

1. **Descriptive anchors** — “EMI calculator” beats “click here.”
2. **Hub and spoke** — category pages link out to tools; tools link back to hubs and sibling tools.
3. **Contextual links in body copy** — not only footers and megamenus.
4. **Fix orphans** — every important URL should have at least a few meaningful inbound internal links.

## Patterns for FreeToolsPro-style sites

- Blog posts link to 2–3 live tools that execute the advice.
- Tool pages link to related tools and a relevant guide.
- New features get links from homepage collections and category indexes—not only from a changelog.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Identical footer spam of hundreds of links on every URL.
- Orphan blog posts that never appear in category hubs.
- Linking only with images that lack accessible names.
- Infinite calendar/filter URLs linked from widgets.

## Editorial habit

When you publish a post, add one link **from** an older related article **to** the new one. Reciprocal discovery beats hoping the sitemap alone will carry you.

Internal linking is architecture you maintain weekly—not a one-time sitewide redesign.
