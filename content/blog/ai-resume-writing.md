AI can turn vague duties into clearer achievement bullets. It cannot invent promotions you never earned—and recruiters notice fiction.

## A truthful AI workflow

1. List real responsibilities, tools, and outcomes (even rough numbers).
2. Draft structure with the [AI Resume Builder](/social-media-tools/ai-resume-builder).
3. Score clarity and impact with the [AI Resume Score](/social-media-tools/ai-resume-score).
4. Soften stiff phrasing via the [AI Humanizer](/social-media-tools/ai-humanizer) if needed.
5. Manually verify every metric and title.

## Bullet formula AI should follow

**Action verb + task + method/tools + measurable result**  
Example: “Automated weekly reports in Python, cutting prep time from 4 hours to 45 minutes.”

If you lack metrics, use scope (“12-person team,” “3 markets”)—not invented revenue.

## Tailoring prompts

“Rewrite these bullets for a [role] emphasizing [skills]. Keep facts unchanged. Prefer verbs over adjectives. Max 2 lines each.”

Refine that instruction with the [AI Prompt Optimizer](/social-media-tools/ai-prompt-optimizer).

## What to avoid

- Keyword stuffing that reads like a bot
- Identical summaries for every application
- Skills you cannot defend in an interview
- Design chaos—clean layout beats fancy templates ATS mishandle

## Pair with applications

Customize the top third of the resume per job family. Keep a master document of true accomplishments as the source of truth.

AI is an editor. You are the author of record—own every line.
