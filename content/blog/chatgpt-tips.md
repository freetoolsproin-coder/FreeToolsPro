ChatGPT is a fast drafting partner. These tips reduce fluffy answers and quiet hallucinations without turning every chat into a research paper.

## Tips that compound

1. **State the format first** — “Reply as a checklist” beats fixing format later.
2. **Paste the source** — policies, syllabi, error logs—then forbid inventing beyond them.
3. **One job per message** — outline, then draft, then edit; don’t ask for all three at once.
4. **Name the audience** — “hiring manager,” “classmate,” “non-technical founder.”
5. **Ask for uncertainty** — “Mark guesses clearly.”

## Follow-up moves

- “Cut 40% without losing the ask.”
- “Make it more concrete; replace adjectives with examples.”
- “Show the diff as bullet changes.”
- “Challenge this plan with risks.”

## Verify important claims

For medical, legal, financial, or citations, treat ChatGPT as a draft. Cross-check primary sources. For originality concerns on school work, see the [AI Content Detector](/trending-tools/ai-content-detector) and your institution’s rules.

## Polish tone

If output feels robotic, run a pass through the [AI Humanizer](/social-media-tools/ai-humanizer)—then edit so it still sounds like you. Tighten the prompt itself with the [AI Prompt Optimizer](/social-media-tools/ai-prompt-optimizer).

## Privacy habits

Don’t paste secrets, full ID numbers, or confidential client data into public chats. Redact first.
