# How to Add Text to a PDF Online

PDF Editor lets you place a text overlay on a selected page. Choose the text, colour, size, transparency, and position, then download the revised PDF.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF Editor tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-editor).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

Keep the original PDF as a reference copy. Review the final page in the downloaded file, especially near margins and existing content.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF Editor tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-editor) and process your PDF in the browser.
