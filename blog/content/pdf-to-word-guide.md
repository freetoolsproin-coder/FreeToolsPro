# How to Convert PDF to Word Online

PDF to Word is useful when you need to edit text from a digitally created PDF. Upload the file, run the conversion, then download the editable Word document. The tool reads the PDF text layer, so it works best when you can select text in the original PDF.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF to Word tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-word).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

Review headings, tables, and page breaks after opening the Word file. Image-only scans need OCR before they can become editable text.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF to Word tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-word) and process your PDF in the browser.
