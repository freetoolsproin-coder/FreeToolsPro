# How to Merge PDF Files Online

PDF Merger combines several files into a single PDF. Add the documents, put them in the right order, and choose page ranges when only part of a file is required.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF Merger tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-merger).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

Confirm the page order before downloading. Keep source files until you have opened the merged document and checked it.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF Merger tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-merger) and process your PDF in the browser.
