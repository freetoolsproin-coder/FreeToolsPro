# How to Extract Telugu Text from a PDF

PDF to Telugu extracts existing Telugu text from a PDF into an editable document. Use it for digitally created PDFs where the Telugu text can already be selected.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF to Telugu tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-telugu).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

This tool preserves existing Telugu text; it does not translate another language into Telugu. Review punctuation and line breaks after conversion.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF to Telugu tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-telugu) and process your PDF in the browser.
