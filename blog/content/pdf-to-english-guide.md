# How to Extract English Text from a PDF

PDF to English extracts existing selectable English text into an editable document. It is a quick option when you need to revise text from a digitally created PDF.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF to English tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-english).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

This tool extracts the existing English text rather than translating another language. Check formatting, headings, and tables after opening the result.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF to English tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-english) and process your PDF in the browser.
