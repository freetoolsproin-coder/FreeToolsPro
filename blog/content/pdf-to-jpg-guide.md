# How to Convert PDF Pages to JPG Images

PDF to JPG turns each page of your document into a separate image. It is useful for sharing a page in chat, creating a preview, or inserting a page into a design.

## Steps

1. Open [the PDF to JPG tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-jpg).
2. Upload the PDF and follow the on-screen options.
3. Download the result and check it before sharing.

## Tips for better results

Check the exported image at full size before sharing. For a multi-page document, download only the pages you need.

## Use the tool

Ready to begin? Open [the PDF to JPG tool](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-jpg) and process your PDF in the browser.
